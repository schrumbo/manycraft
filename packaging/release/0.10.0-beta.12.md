Sync gets simple, chat lines reach every account, sign-ins retry on their own, and headless accounts learn a lot. Still a beta: it installs over your Manycraft and keeps your data.

- Sync: a one-time setup picks the instance whose files everyone starts from; then every instance shares options, mod settings, worlds and screenshots, each with its own switch. An instance can opt out in its settings. History, base switching and server list sharing are gone
- Lines and commands sent to a batch reach every account: accounts that are starting, waiting their turn or reconnecting get the line once they are in, after their join commands; lines still waiting after two minutes are dropped and say so
- Microsoft sign-ins that fail for a moment (busy servers, timeouts, rate limits) retry each step on their own and the start tries again later; dead logins still never retry
- Maps in item frames show their real picture in headless screenshots
- New instance header: name, state, version, play time, "Ordner öffnen", account and Spielen in one calm block, tabs on its bottom edge
- Server menus of headless accounts show real item pictures with tooltips; Shift-click moves a stack, right-click takes half or places one
- Headless accounts show health and hunger on their tile, flag other players nearby, respawn on their own after death (switch per batch) and offer Wiederbeleben, hotbar slot and Gegenstand benutzen
- Batch chat goes to the selected tiles, or to every account when none is selected; the field says who gets it
- Headless screenshots: switch off chat, hotbar, hand, crosshair, bars, boss bars, scoreboard, titles or vignette in Settings
- Headless accounts answer server resource packs as the batch says and sign chat on servers that enforce secure chat
- Sync page opens the shared folders and shows where the files live; files placed there apply at the next start
- Tooltips on buttons that have their own hover state show again
- Play time per instance in its header and in total on Start
- Account menu: add and manage stay pinned at the bottom, the list scrolls and grows to the top of the window; Wardrobe left the menu
- Settings regrouped into eight clear groups without "Erweitert"
- Instance settings use the full width, memory takes typed gigabytes, the top result card stays inside its list
- "Nach Updates suchen" button on the Mods tab
- Faster starts: no network wait when the game files are already there, mod files are hashed only when they changed, a game you play alone starts with higher priority on Windows
- Discord shows what you play: "3 accounts gooning in Farm" for batches, the instance and version when you play alone; switch it off in Settings
- "Aus Datei hinzufügen…" on Mods, resource packs and shaders: pick files from your computer, a taken name asks before replacing
- Project pages show SVG badges and pictures that stayed blank before (e.g. ImmediatelyFast)
- Headless accounts: light, the Nether and the End look right, boss bars show their names, and chunks no longer break after a dimension change
- Scrolling long lists in Discover is lighter: repeated text is no longer laid out again
- Fixed a crash when zooming in the screenshot viewer, and secured 22 more places where a closed page could crash the app

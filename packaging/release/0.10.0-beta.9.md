Headless screenshots that look like the game, one shared file for options and mod settings, and a larger screenshot viewer. Still a beta: it installs over your Manycraft and keeps your data.

- Headless screenshots show players with their skins, armor and held items, your own hand, mobs and animals, villagers, item frames, armor stands, dropped items and block and item displays, lit like the game, with time of day and weather, at 1280x720
- Double-click a headless tile to take a screenshot; "Screenshots ansehen" opens them large, fit or 1:1, and the arrow keys switch between eye view, map and earlier shots
- Sharing keeps one real file: instances that share options, mod settings or the server list all read and change the same one instead of copies. Before every start Manycraft saves the shared state and puts it back if a mod or a fresh game resets it, with a note. Each switch says in words what it does, including what waits for a running game to close. Existing shared instances move over on the next start; their old files stay under "Frühere Stände"
- Discover and Add content use the full width
- The macOS download is back

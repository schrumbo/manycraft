Login protection, reliable batch chat, a Sync page and headless screenshots that stay in memory. Still a beta: it installs over your Manycraft and keeps your data.

- "Anmeldung vor Mods verbergen", per instance and off by default: the game only gets a stand-in, the real sign-in stays in Manycraft, and a proxy also stays out of the game's command line. Not for Realms or versions before 1.16. Quitting or updating while protected games run says that they lose their sign-in
- "Alle Accounts abmelden" in Settings signs every account out at once
- Chat sent to a batch is paced so servers don't kick for spam, and says how many accounts got it and which did not, with the reason
- Sync has its own page under Batches: pick the instance to copy from, the instances that take it over and what is shared; new instances join on their own unless switched off, and every replace can be undone
- Headless screenshots stay in memory and are never written to the instance folder; joining or changing worlds takes one on its own. "Speichern unter …" and "Bild kopieren" keep one when you want it
- Headless pictures show your own hand and held item like the game, filled maps in item frames, the HUD (hotbar, health, food, scoreboard, boss bars, titles, chat), and name tags with team colours
- Settings use the full width, Discover's list and details end at the same edge, READMEs with HTML render properly, and a project's gallery opens as a grid with a large viewer
- Drop a modpack file anywhere on the window to import it

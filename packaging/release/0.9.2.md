Closing a game closes it.

- Closing a game window (X or Alt+F4) ends that game and saves its world; Manycraft comes back with the tile selected and offers "Rückgängig" for 10 seconds
- Stopping one game gives it up to 30 seconds to save; after 5 seconds "Sofort beenden" appears on the tile, in its menu, the palette and the instance page
- Games still waiting for memory or installing stop at once; stopping the game you are playing brings the grid back right away
- The first time you play a game, it shows how to close it and how to get back to the grid
- Ctrl+. works on the instance page even when the window is not focused

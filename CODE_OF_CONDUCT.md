# Code of Conduct

## What Manycraft is

Manycraft starts, shows and organises Minecraft clients you own. It never plays for you: it generates no movement, no input and no chat of its own. It only sends what you wrote yourself, such as chat you type and the join commands you set.

## What Manycraft is not

- No macros, bots, auto-clickers or scripts that play the game, and no hooks or APIs meant for them.
- Nothing that hides accounts from a server, dodges its limits or bans, or fakes being a single player.
- No list of servers that allow alts, and no claim that any server does.

Feature requests, pull requests and support questions for any of this get closed without discussion.

## Your responsibility

You decide which accounts you run, where and how. The rules of each server, the [Minecraft EULA](https://www.minecraft.net/eula) and the laws where you live apply to you, not to Manycraft. Some servers allow AFK accounts or tools of their own; whether you use those is your choice and your risk. Settings in Manycraft, such as how much a hidden instance is slowed down, exist so the app fits how you play, not to make anything allowed that is not.

## How we treat each other

In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content or personal attacks, and no posting other people's private data.
- Keep it on topic and in English or German.
- Never share tokens, passwords, account names or logs with personal data. Remove them before posting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions and block people who break these rules, with or without a warning. Report problems by opening an issue that only says you want to report something, or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
